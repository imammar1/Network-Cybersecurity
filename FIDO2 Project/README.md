# Evaluating the Viability of FIDO2 Authentication in University Environments
**A Survey-Based Exploration** | University of Michigan—Dearborn

---

## Overview

This research project evaluates whether FIDO2/WebAuthn passwordless authentication is a viable alternative to traditional password-based systems in university environments. Through a structured survey study, we assessed student awareness, adoption attitudes, and accessibility concerns surrounding passkey technology on college campuses.

---

## Research Questions

1. How do students perceive the current authentication methods at their universities?
2. Are students aware of FIDO2 passwordless authentication?
3. What thoughts and concerns do students have with FIDO2's use for campus resources?
4. Are there accessibility concerns for students if they were required to use passkeys?

---

## Methodology

- **Survey Platform:** Qualtrics
- **Distribution Period:** 3 weeks
- **Participants:** 40 college students across 23 universities
- **Approach:** Mixed-methods (quantitative + qualitative)
- **Survey Sections:**
  - Demographics
  - Current Authentication Practices & Awareness
  - Mobile/Roaming Authenticator Experience
  - FIDO2/Passkey Awareness & Adoption Attitudes

---

## Key Findings

### Current Authentication Practices
- **95%** of participants use passwords as their primary authentication method
- **85%** have MFA implemented at their university
- **85%** rated their current authentication methods as easy to use
- **75%+** reported receiving phishing attempts, with 32.5% receiving them more than once a month

### FIDO2 Awareness
| Familiarity Level | Participants | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Very familiar | 2 | 5% |
| Heard of it | 13 | 32.5% |
| Never heard of it | 25 | 62.5% |

- Of the 13 who had heard of FIDO2, fewer than 5 demonstrated accurate understanding
- Common misconceptions included thinking passkeys were cloud passcodes or just another form of MFA

### Adoption Attitudes
- **62.5%** wanted their university to at least be aware of FIDO2 as an option
- **35%** wanted their university to actively look into adopting it
- **72.5%** were comfortable using passkeys on personal devices for university authentication

### Top Concerns with FIDO2 Adoption
| Concern | Participants | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Ease of use | 28 | 70% |
| Personal privacy | 22 | 55% |
| Cost of implementation | 13 | 32.5% |
| Access to authenticators | 11 | 27.5% |
| Increased security risk | 7 | 17.5% |

### Accessibility
- **82.5%** believe universities should provide an alternative for those uncomfortable using personal devices
- **77.5%** believe universities should provide authentication devices for students without access to personal devices

---

## Recommendations

1. **Feasibility Studies** — Universities should conduct internal feasibility studies to identify technical and policy roadblocks before considering adoption
2. **User Education** — Significant education efforts are needed given the widespread misconceptions about how FIDO2 works and what data it stores
3. **Phased Rollout** — An optional adoption phase would ease students into passkey use and address ease-of-use concerns
4. **Accessibility Planning** — Any implementation must include alternative authentication options and university-provided devices for students without personal devices
